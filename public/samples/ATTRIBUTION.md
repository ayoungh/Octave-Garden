# Salamander Grand Piano

Piano recordings by **Alexander Holm**, Salamander Grand Piano, Yamaha C5.

Licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution 3.0**: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Source: https://github.com/Tonejs/audio/tree/master/salamander

The 30 MP3 samples bundled here are the Tone.js example's reduced sample set. They are hosted locally, pitch-shifted between sampled notes, and played with velocity-controlled gain. The original recordings have not been modified by this project. Tone.js is MIT licensed.
