Built with a little help.

Octave Garden is an independent, local piano-learning experiment. Its code and authored lessons are available under the MIT licence.

The piano you hear

Salamander Grand Piano, recordings by Alexander Holm of a Yamaha C5, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0. The 30 MP3 files are distributed via Tone.js audio and bundled unchanged here. Playback changes pitch and gain to respond to notes and velocity.

The music

Hot Cross Buns, Mary Had a Little Lamb and the opening theme of Beethoven's Ode to Joy use simplified, newly entered beginner arrangements of traditional or historical melodies. No commercial recordings or lyrics are included.
